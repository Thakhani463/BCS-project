# ☕ BCS Project  
## Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis  

---

## 📊 Project Overview  
This project analyses sales data from a coffee shop business to understand:  
- Revenue performance  
- Customer behaviour  
- Product demand  

The goal is to identify key trends, compare store performance, and generate actionable insights to support business decision-making.  

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Analyse overall sales performance across stores  
- Identify key drivers of revenue  
- Understand customer purchasing patterns  
- Provide data-driven recommendations  

---

## 🗂️ Data Description  
The dataset consists of transactional sales data, including:  
- Transaction date and time  
- Product category and product details  
- Store location  
- Quantity sold  
- Unit price  

**Revenue Calculation:**  Revenue = Quantity × Unit Price


---

## ⚙️ Methodology  

### 🔹 Data Processing  
- Data processed using SQL in Databricks  
- Created new variables (e.g., revenue, time buckets)  
- Grouped data by date, store, product, and time  

### 🔹 Data Analysis  
- Data exported to Excel  
- Pivot tables used for summarisation  

**Key metrics analysed:**  
- Revenue  
- Quantity sold  
- Store performance  

---

## 📈 Data Visualisation  
Charts created include:  
- Monthly revenue trend  
- Revenue by store  
- Revenue contribution (pie chart)  
- Revenue by time of day  
- Revenue by day of week  
- Product category performance  
- Top and bottom products  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  
- Revenue increased significantly from January to June, indicating strong growth  
- Morning hours generated the highest sales (peak customer activity)  
- Revenue was evenly distributed across stores  
- Coffee and tea products dominated revenue and quantity sold  
- A small number of products contributed most of the revenue  
- Some products showed very low performance and may require review
